ABOUT THE PROJECT

About us.

Making it easier to understand what Congress is doing on AI safety—and ask for the next step.

From concern to a concrete ask

Congress Directory brings together information about members of the House and Senate, sourced legislative actions, and specific AI safety calls to action.

You can explore a proposal, see which actions have been documented, and prepare a friendly message to a representative or senator. Requests are a starting point to review and personalize before sending.

Who’s behind it

This is a personal project by Felix Moses. For now, Felix is the sole person behind the project.

A record we’re still building

The directory reflects explicit AI safety advocacy goals. Its research is incomplete, and missing evidence means a position has not been established in our records.

Documented legislative actions link to their sources so you can inspect the record yourself. Older actions remain visible with historical labels. The roster is a dated snapshot.
